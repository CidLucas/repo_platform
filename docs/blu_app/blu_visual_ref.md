Blu by DeepBlue — Visual Reference Guideline

1. Brand Foundation
   1.1 Brand Story
   DeepBlue builds Blu — an AI bureau for Brazilian business owners.
   The ocean is deep, calm, and powerful. The sky is vast, clear, and open. Blue is trust without stiffness, depth without darkness, modernity without coldness.
   Blu inherits this: calm power. The interface feels like a deep breath. The user is in control. The agents are competent. The experience is clear.
   1.2 Personality
   Table
   Trait Expression
   Calm No panic, no urgency in the interface. Decisions are framed as opportunities, not crises.
   Powerful Deep capabilities surfaced simply. The user feels capable, not overwhelmed.
   Accessible No jargon. No hidden features. Everything is discoverable.
   Professional Precise, reliable, consistent. The interface never lies.
   Humane Warm accents, friendly microcopy, acknowledgment of effort.
   1.3 Voice
   "Você decide. A gente cuida do resto."
   "Sugestões com explicação. Aprovação com você."
   "Quanto mais a gente aprende sobre o seu negócio, mais útil a gente fica."
   Never: "Transforme," "Revolucione," "10x," "Magia da IA."
